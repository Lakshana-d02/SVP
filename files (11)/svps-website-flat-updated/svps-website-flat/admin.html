Customer Enquiries

Every enquiry a customer sends is saved automatically into your own private Google Sheet - no server, no login here, no technical setup to maintain. Only you can see it, using your own Google account.

Haven't set this up yet? Open the google-sheet-setup folder in your website files and follow SETUP-INSTRUCTIONS.txt - it takes about 5 minutes and needs no coding.
