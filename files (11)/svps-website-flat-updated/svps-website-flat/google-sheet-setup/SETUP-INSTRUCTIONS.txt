HOW TO GET CUSTOMER ENQUIRIES INTO A GOOGLE SHEET
==================================================
No coding, no server, no terminal. Takes about 5 minutes, once.
You only ever need YOUR Google account (Gmail) - nothing to install.

STEP 1 - Create the sheet
--------------------------
1. Go to https://sheets.google.com
2. Click the "+" (Blank spreadsheet).
3. Rename it to something like "SVP Systems - Customer Enquiries"
   (click the title at the top left to rename).

STEP 2 - Add the script
------------------------
1. In the Google Sheet, click "Extensions" in the top menu.
2. Click "Apps Script". A new tab opens with a code editor.
3. Delete anything already in the box (usually says "function myFunction() {}").
4. Open the file "apps-script-code.gs" (included alongside this file),
   copy ALL of its contents, and paste it into that box.
5. Click the disk/save icon (or press Ctrl+S).

STEP 3 - Publish it as a web link
-----------------------------------
1. Still in the Apps Script tab, click the blue "Deploy" button (top right)
   -> "New deployment".
2. Click the gear/settings icon next to "Select type" and choose "Web app".
3. Fill in:
     Description:        Customer enquiries
     Execute as:          Me (your email)
     Who has access:      Anyone
   (Don't worry - "Anyone" just means the website can send data IN.
    Nobody can read your sheet without logging into your Google account.)
4. Click "Deploy".
5. Google may ask you to "Authorize access" - click through with your
   own Google account and click "Allow" (you'll see a warning screen
   saying "Google hasn't verified this app" - this is normal for your
   own private script; click "Advanced" -> "Go to (your project name),
   unsafe" -> "Allow").
6. You'll now see a "Web app URL" - it looks like:
     https://script.google.com/macros/s/AKfycb.../exec
   COPY this link.

STEP 4 - Connect it to the website
-------------------------------------
1. Open the website file "assets/app.js" in any text editor (Notepad
   works fine).
2. Find this line near the top:
     const BACKEND_URL = "";
3. Paste your link between the quotes, so it looks like:
     const BACKEND_URL = "https://script.google.com/macros/s/AKfycb.../exec";
4. Save the file and re-upload it to your website (replacing the old one).

That's it! From now on, every enquiry submitted on the website's
Enquiry page appears as a new row in your Google Sheet automatically.

HOW TO VIEW YOUR CUSTOMER LIST
================================
Just open https://sheets.google.com any time, on your phone or
computer, and open your "SVP Systems - Customer Enquiries" sheet.
To download it as an Excel file: File -> Download -> Microsoft Excel (.xlsx)

WHO CAN SEE THIS DATA?
========================
Only you - anyone signed into your Google account that owns the
sheet. It is private by default. Customers browsing the website
never see it, and it never appears anywhere on the site itself.
