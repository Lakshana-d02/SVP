About Sri Vinayaga Power System

Your local shop for inverters, batteries and RO water purifiers in Nandhivaram, Guduvanchery.

Straightforward answers, quick and simple

SVP Systems (Sri Vinayaga Power System) has been helping homes around Guduvanchery stay powered through outages and keep their drinking water safe. We pair the right inverter with a battery based on your actual daily load, and fit multi-stage RO+UV+UF purifiers with advanced external filtration for hard or tanker-fed water. From new installation to repairs and routine service, our technicians handle it on-site - and every conversation starts on WhatsApp.
